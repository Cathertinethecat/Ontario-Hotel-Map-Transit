About the data

Hotels.

  • 40 open hotels, 5,049 rooms counted, of 5,461 citywide; 1,800+ planned [1].
  • Room counts as of October 2026, each cited on its card.
  • Hotel roster and addresses from GOCAL [4]; positions placed by address.
  • ONT courtesy-shuttle badges from the airport’s list [5]; nightly rate ranges are a snapshot [6].
  • Each city's hotels gather into one dot placed at the center of its city limits (Chino's sits at its hotels); tap a dot to spread them.
  • Hotels in the other cities, from El Monte and the east San Gabriel Valley through Pomona, east to Yucaipa and south to Corona, Perris and Moreno Valley, come from directory listings [7] [8] [9] with room counts and meeting space checked against each hotel's official site or a meetings directory (Cvent, Meetings &
  • Conventions, Northstar) in October 2026 and cited on the card, then audited link by link (109 of 141 room counts and 83 meeting figures confirmed on an official or meetings-industry page; the rest rest on the directory alone); several have rebranded since the directories were compiled and the card says so.
  • Placed by address and approximate; they are dash-outlined on the map.

Boundaries and transit.

  • City limits are the official boundaries from the Board of Equalization layer, loaded live; gray hatched areas are unincorporated county land, computed from the county boundary minus every incorporated city [10].
  • Metro rail geometry from LA Metro’s GTFS feed, stitched to the 2025 network [11];
  • Metrolink lines placed by station, approximate [12].
  • No rail reaches ONT or the hotel district; nearest stations are Metrolink East Ontario and Rancho Cucamonga, and the A Line ends at Pomona North.
  • No shared hotel shuttle: 14 hotels run their own vans to ONT (badged on cards);
  • Omnitrans Route 61 links Holt Blvd, ONT and Ontario Mills every 20 minutes but skips the Convention Center [16].
  • West Valley Connector facts from SBCTA and Omnitrans [17] [18];
  • Sports Empire program from the City and the rendering from the designer [3] [19].

LA28.

  • Venues and sports from la28.org: all 41 venues in the LA region plus San Diego Stadium (soccer semifinals and bronze matches); the other soccer stadiums, softball and slalom are out of state and not shown [13].
  • Competition dates, days and sessions per venue from the Competition Schedule by Event, v4.0, subject to change; medal sessions are those LA28 types Final or Bronze; late sessions end at 22:00 or later [14].
  • Spectator capacities are a proxy compiled from operator and reported figures [15];
  • LA28 has published none, and temporary venues show “not yet published”.

Connectors.

  • Each corridor line cites its own sources on its card.
